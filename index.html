About me. Engineering meets data.

Background

I earned my B.S. in Civil Engineering from Florida Atlantic University and started my career as a transportation engineer, writing Smart Work Zone plans, deploying traffic cameras, and building my first Power BI reports from work zone device data. Today I work at Prime AE Group, a transportation engineering consulting firm, where data analytics has become the core of what I do.

My goal

I enjoy building solutions that do more than display data. My goal is to create reliable, automated reporting that clearly explains what is happening, why it is happening, and where improvements are needed, whether for a county's road network or a company's bottom line.

What. Why. Where next.

What I do

Most of my day is building dashboards from scratch. I call APIs to pull the data, clean and transform it with Python and Power Query, store it in Azure Blob Storage, model it into a star schema, and design the visuals in Power BI, then automate the refresh so reports stay current without manual work. For transportation clients that means corridor performance, travel-time reliability, device health, and Smart Work Zone monitoring, with ArcGIS handling the spatial side. For our corporate team I apply the same process to executive KPI dashboards covering revenue, AR aging, sales, and project management metrics. I also get out in the field, setting up cameras at intersections to record traffic flow, which is then processed into turning-movement and volume counts. Throughout all of it, I rely heavily on AI tools to write and debug code, speed up data transformation, and prototype new ideas faster.
